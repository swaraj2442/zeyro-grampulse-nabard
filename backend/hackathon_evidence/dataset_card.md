# Dataset Card: GramPulse Synthetic Panel v1.0

## Dataset Summary
A highly realistic, causally constrained synthetic enterprise panel generated using the GramPulse simulator. It spans 5,000 unique rural enterprises across Maharashtra over 36 months, strictly adhering to double-entry accounting rules and bounded by ASUSE/AIDIS official quantiles.

## Supported Tasks
- Time-series Cash Flow Forecasting
- Early Warning Systems for MSMEs
- Underwriting Risk Classification
- Large Language Model Fine-Tuning (via the companion `language_corpus.parquet`)
