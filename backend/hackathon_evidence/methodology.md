# Methodology: GramPulse

## 1. Distribution Calibration
We extract bounds and relationships from public surveys (ASUSE for operating ratios, AIDIS for debt burden, UDISE/AGMARKNET for locality data) without using PII.

## 2. Causal Simulation
Enterprises are rolled forward through a rigorous double-entry accounting engine. Inflow is causally linked to assets, market prices, and climate shocks.

## 3. Machine Learning Hardening
CatBoost models are trained progressively (Financial -> Credit -> Digital -> Market -> Climate) on strictly partitioned out-of-fold data to prevent look-ahead bias and ensure genuine structural generalisation.
