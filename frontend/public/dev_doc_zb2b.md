# Credit Underwriting

Credit underwriting is the process of evaluating whether a borrower is likely to repay a loan and determining the level of risk involved in extending credit.

Every lending institution has its own underwriting process, risk policies, and approval criteria. While these may differ across organizations, they all rely on one thing—having enough financial information to make informed decisions.

Zeyro helps enrich the underwriting process by transforming financial data into financial intelligence that underwriters and AI systems can use to better understand a borrower.

Rather than replacing existing underwriting systems, Zeyro integrates alongside them, providing additional context and AI-powered insights to support lending decisions.

## Why underwriting needs more than a credit score

Traditional underwriting often relies on historical information such as credit bureau reports, financial statements, income verification, and repayment history.

While these remain important, they only represent part of a borrower's financial profile.

For example, two businesses may have similar credit histories but very different financial behaviour today. One may have healthy and consistent cash flow, while the other may be experiencing declining revenues or irregular payment patterns.

Understanding this broader financial context allows institutions to make lending decisions with greater confidence.

## A typical underwriting scenario

Imagine a small business applying for a working capital loan.

As part of the application, the lender reviews information such as:

- Credit bureau reports
- Banking transactions
- GST filings
- Accounting records
- Financial statements
- Supporting documents

Instead of reviewing each source independently, Zeyro combines this information into a unified financial intelligence layer.

AI can then identify patterns, highlight potential risks, summarize key financial indicators, and provide supporting insights that help underwriters complete their assessment more efficiently.

The final lending decision always remains with the institution.

## How Zeyro helps

Zeyro supports the underwriting process by helping organizations:

- Combine financial information from multiple sources.
- Build a unified understanding of every borrower.
- Generate AI-assisted underwriting insights.
- Provide explainable recommendations with supporting context.
- Deliver intelligence directly into existing underwriting workflows.

This allows institutions to enhance existing processes without replacing their current loan origination or underwriting systems.

## Common applications

Credit underwriting powered by Zeyro can support a wide range of lending products, including:

- Consumer lending
- MSME lending
- Commercial lending
- Working capital finance
- Supply chain finance
- Loan renewals
- Credit reviews

Because every application builds on the same financial intelligence, organizations can deliver more consistent lending decisions across different products and business units.
